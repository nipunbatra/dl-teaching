# Whole-deck visual review

Reviewed locally on 19 October 2026 against the actual Beyond Attention / From Attention to Applications lecture in `../attention-followups/transformer-outputs/`.

All **71 scenes** were reviewed: 60 core scenes and 11 appendix scenes. The result contains **65 editable vector figures/compositions and six interactive demonstrations**. This pass includes the original 26 revised scenes as well as applications, code, experiments and appendix mathematics.

## Presentation rules

- Large evidence or a concrete construction carries the explanation. Arrows identify operations; semantic colours distinguish image state, conditions, noise and predictions.
- The visible strip keeps a speaking cue on earlier builds and a takeaway on the last build. The two-page recap and full instructor notes remain available.
- Explanatory paragraphs no longer repeat below the figure. Original build explanations and derivations are retained in Notes. Unique equations remain beside the visual; repeated equation carry-over is hidden in reading mode.
- Published outputs, actual local samples and illustrative diagrams retain distinct labels. Source images remain intact; the compositions use explicit clipped viewports.

## Verification

All 191 build states were rendered. The 65 SVG scenes were checked across their 173 reveal states: no detected label intersections or text outside the SVG. Browser checks found no overflow, failed images, duplicate IDs, mathematical rendering errors or JavaScript errors. Six demos passed reset/play/pause/leave checks. The new mode checks cover guidance endpoints, the second attention query, masked-loss alignment, completed scripted generation and recursive corruption. No complete takeaway sentence is duplicated in the visible figure/body. Geometry checks complement the visual review; they are not a visual-quality score.

Completed scenes were inspected on six contact sheets, with detailed inspection of source crops, code, derivations and the revised demo modes. Both lecture PDFs were regenerated from the final captures and independently rendered for review.
